Slotcars

Draw your own track along real city streets, then race toy slot cars round it.

One thumb per driver, up to four on the same screen. Hold to go, lift to brake. Take a corner too fast and the car pops out of its slot. Share your tracks with friends to challenge them to beat your time or race up to 4 players on the same screen in local multiplayer.

Kind
Racing game for 1–4 players on one screen
Built with
Flutter, OpenStreetMap data, Cloudflare Pages for downloadable maps, and Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics
Runs on
iOS, Android and the web

Patchlands

A puzzle game about filling a field, one oddly shaped vegetable patch at a time.

Pick a patch, pay for it in coins and time, and find the spot where it fits. There is a solo campaign, daily challenges and local or online multiplayer.

Kind
Puzzle game, solo and multiplayer
Built with
Flutter, with a local database so it plays offline and Firebase for online games
Runs on
iOS and Android

Patchwise

The same puzzle as Patchlands with the farm taken out.

Plain wooden tiles, a quiet palette and a dark mode, for anyone who wants the puzzle and nothing around it. It shares its game engine with Patchlands and is a study in how far the same rules can be restyled.

my friend from

A private address book for the friends you make while travelling: who they are, where you met and where they live now.

It keeps everything on your phone and works without a signal. Nothing is shared unless you show someone your QR code. Built with Flutter.

Early build

Nutrimons

A monster-collecting game that starts in the grocery aisle.

Scan a food barcode to hatch a monster shaped by the product nutrients. Build a dex, raise a team and challenge a friend to a duel. Built with Flutter.

Fikakassan

A minimal PoS system for club kiosks and bake sales.

Tap what the customer wants, show them the total and a Swish QR code, and serve the next one. The big picture buttons make it easy enough for a child to run the kiosk. It is in Swedish, works offline and keeps everything on the device.
