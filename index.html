Slotcars

Trace a loop through the real streets of London, Stockholm or New York, then race toy slot cars round it.

One thumb per driver, up to four on the same screen. Hold to go, lift to brake, and take a corner too fast and the car pops out of its slot. On your own it is a time trial against your best lap.

Kind
Racing game for 1–4 players on one screen
Built with
Flutter and OpenStreetMap data, packed small enough to ship three cities

Patchlands

A puzzle game about filling a field, one oddly shaped vegetable patch at a time.

Pick a patch, pay for it in coins and time, and find the spot where it fits. There is a solo campaign, a survival mode and multiplayer against a friend, started with an invite link.

Kind
Puzzle game, solo and multiplayer
Built with
Flutter, with a local database so it plays offline and Firebase for online games
Runs on
iPhone, iPad and Android

Patchwise

The same puzzle as Patchlands with the farm taken out.

Plain wooden tiles, a quiet palette and a dark mode, for anyone who wants the puzzle and nothing around it. It shares its game engine with Patchlands and is a study in how far the same rules can be restyled.

my friend from

A private address book for the friends you make while travelling: who they are, where you met and where they live now.

It keeps everything on your phone and works without a signal. Nothing is shared unless you show someone your QR code. Built with Flutter.
