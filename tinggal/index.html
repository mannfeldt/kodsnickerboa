How it works

One match, seen from both sides.

Tinggal works like a matchmaker. Each side sets things up once, and a home and a person only meet when the details line up.

If you're renting or buying

  1. Describe the home you want

    Areas, budget, rent or buy, bedrooms, lease length and the things you won't compromise on. It takes a few minutes, once.

  2. Get on with your life

    There is no feed to scroll. Tinggal checks every new listing against your home profile.

  3. Say interested or pass

    A match arrives with photos, location and price. One tap either way, and a pass stays between you and the app.

If you're a landlord, seller or agent

  1. List the home once

    Photos, map location, price and terms. One listing, no reposting and no bumping.

  2. Tinggal finds the people it fits

    Your listing goes to the people whose home profile it matches, and to nobody else.

  3. See who said yes

    You get a notification for each interested person, with their profile attached.

Then you talk directly

The owner or agent gets in touch with the interested person. Questions, viewing, contract: that part is between the two of you.

    Questions people ask first

    Do I need to be moving soon?

    No. Tinggal is made for people who are keeping an eye out. Set up your profile, carry on, and you'll hear from us only when a home fits. If you are in a hurry the same profile works, you'll just say interested more often.

    Who contacts whom?

    The owner, seller or agent contacts the person looking. When you mark a match as interested, they get a notification with your profile and can reach out directly. If you pass, they never hear about it.

    Can I use Tinggal to buy a home?

    Yes. A home profile can be for renting, buying or both. Listings for sale state whether the home is leasehold or freehold, and how many years are left on a lease.

    I'm an agent with many listings. Is this for me?

    Yes. Agents list on behalf of owners and see the interest for each home. Every person who reaches you has already seen the price, location and terms, and said yes.

    Is Tinggal only for Bali?

    Bali comes first because that is where the search is hardest. The matching isn't tied to one place, and new areas open as listings and home profiles arrive there.
